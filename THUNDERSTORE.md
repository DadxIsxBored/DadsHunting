# Thunderstore release

Run `./build.ps1 -Package`, then upload the ZIP in `dist/` under the `Dad_Is_Bored` namespace. The ZIP contains `DadsHunting.dll`, `manifest.json`, `README.md`, `icon.png`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `LICENSE`, and `SOURCES.md` at its root.

Before release, keep the version in `DadsHunting.csproj`, `src/DadsHuntingPlugin.cs`, and `package/manifest.json` aligned.
